Aktuelt-kort — the editorial teaser used in threes across the Aktuelt grid.

**Intentional addition.** The Figma kit does not name a symbol `ArticleCard`; this component is a confirmed intentional addition, built from Aktuelt grid cards in `Aars_Forsiden.v.2` and `Aars_Aktuelt_Desktop-1440`.

```jsx
<ArticleCard image="/assets/images/mollersamlingen-utstilling.jpg" kicker="170 år med fotografi fra Møllersamlingen" title="Eksponert: Utstilling Henie Onstad Kunstsenter" />
```

384 wide at desktop, 384×251 image, kicker at 20/30, title at 28/42, "Les mer" underneath. No card border, no shadow, no radius — the image edge is the card.
