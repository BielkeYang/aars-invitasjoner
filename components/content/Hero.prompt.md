Full-bleed hero — photograph under a warm 25% scrim, one centred beige sentence, one outline button. Opens the front page and every section landing page.

**Intentional addition.** The Figma kit does not name a symbol `Hero`; this component is a confirmed intentional addition, built from the hero block of `Aars_Forsiden.v.2_Desktop-1440` and `Aars_Om-Aars.v.1_Desktop-1440`.

```jsx
<Hero image="/assets/images/hero-forsiden.jpg" headline="I over 85 år har vi skapt arbeidsplasser og bidratt til å løse samfunnsutfordringer gjennom aktivt eierskap." cta="Les mer om Aars" />
```

Headline is 48/62 on desktop, max 1007px wide. Never place type on an unscrimmed photograph.
